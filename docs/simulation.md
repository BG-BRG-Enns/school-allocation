# Solver simulation

Generated by `npm run simulate` (e2e/scenario/simulate.ts): 5 schools drawn by the scenario generator from seeds 20331107, 20331108, 20331109, 20331110, 20331111, each loaded into a throwaway database through the services as its people would act, closed by the clock and allocated by the real solver (OR-Tools 9.15.6755, solver build dev) with 30 s of deterministic time per level. Times are wall-clock on the machine that ran it. The database draws a school's ids anew at every load, and the solver's request lists students and activities in id order; where the deterministic time stops a level the solver has not proven depends on that order, so two loads of one seed can come out quite differently. A stored run repeated with its seed answers the same.

Every school: 600 students in 25 classes, 60 teachers, 69 activities over three project days; 3 wishes per time group; a daily minimum of 100 minutes for grade 5, 100 minutes for grade 6, 100 minutes for grade 7, 100 minutes for grade 8, 200 minutes for grade 9, 200 minutes for grade 10, 200 minutes for grade 11; 20% places beyond the need as the demand marker's target; the rules "Sport ≥ 1 activity, grades 5–11" and "Berufsorientierung ≥ 200 minutes, grade 7".
