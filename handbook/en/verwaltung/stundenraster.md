# Timetable

The timetable contains the school's periods with their start and end times. Teachers use it to enter their sessions (“from period 2, lasting 4 periods”), and SchulWahl works out the clock times. They enter sessions at other times, such as a trip, by clock time.

![The timetable: the start and end of each period, followed by the Add period, Remove last and Save buttons](/bilder/en/stundenraster.png)

Change the start and end times, use **Add period** to add a period at the end or **Remove last** to remove the last one, and click **Save**. SchulWahl checks that:

- The periods are numbered from 1 without gaps.
- Every clock time is in HH:MM format.
- Every period ends after it starts.
- No period starts before the previous one ends.

Sessions already entered keep their clock times if you change the timetable later.
