# Administration

The administration sets up SchulWahl for the school and keeps it up to date: who is at the school, who may do what, who leads each class, the periods of the timetable, the language and how long data is kept. The organisation manages the registrations themselves; the [Organisation](/en/organisation/) handbook explains how. The administration may do everything the organisation may do.

## Signing in for the first time

The operator decides who gets the first administration role during setup: they enter your school address in the server settings. You get the administration role when you first sign in, and the **Administration** link appears at the top. You grant every further role in SchulWahl itself, under [Accounts and roles](./konten-und-rollen). If the operator later removes your address, you keep the role; it can only be revoked in SchulWahl.

## Before the first registration

Before the organisation creates the first registration:

1. Choose the school's **Language** (below).
2. Import and apply the [class lists](./klassenlisten).
3. Check the [timetable](./stundenraster): teachers use it to enter their sessions.
4. Assign the [class teachers](./klassenvorstaende), unless the class list already names them.
5. Give the people organising registrations the **Organisation** role ([Accounts and roles](./konten-und-rollen)).
6. Look through the [email templates](/en/organisation/e-mail-vorlagen) and send yourself a test email. If it does not arrive, sending email is not configured on the server; that is the operator's responsibility.

## The school's language

SchulWahl uses one language for everyone at a school: on the pages, in the Excel files and in the emails.

- **German (informal)** addresses everyone as du, as colleagues at a school do.
- **German (formal for teachers and administration)** addresses staff as Sie and students as du. It uses the German handbooks.
- **English** uses British English.

Choose the language under **School language** and save; the page then reloads in the new language. Email templates that someone at the school has changed stay as they are. Their page flags each one written in another language: translate it or reset it.
