# Retention

Project week data is kept until one year after the end of its school year. You can then delete it here, with a reason. SchulWahl deletes nothing automatically.

![The Retention page: school years with their registrations, imports and class teachers and how long they are kept, followed by the Delete school year form](/bilder/en/aufbewahrung.png)

## The school years

The table shows every school year with data: its registrations and their status, the class list imports, the class teachers and how long the data is kept. Once the retention period has passed, it says “expired”. People with the **Read-only** role can view this page too.

## Deleting a school year

You can delete a school year once its retention period has passed and all its registrations are closed. Until then, the selection field tells you why you cannot delete it yet.

1. First save what the school needs to keep, such as the allocation with the cost as an Excel file for the accounts department ([Excel files](/en/organisation/excel)).
2. Choose the **School year** and enter it again to confirm.
3. Write a **Reason** and click **Delete permanently**.

SchulWahl then deletes everything for that school year: the registrations with their choices, allocations and attendance, the class list imports, the class teachers and the teachers' availability on its days. The page then shows what was deleted. Deletion is permanent; there is no way back.
