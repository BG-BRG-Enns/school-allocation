# During registration

## Opening

Registration opens automatically at the **Start** set in the [settings](./einstellungen), or immediately with **Open registration**. When it opens, each class activity takes in its whole classes, and all participating students receive the invitation email.

## What students see

![The My choices page: time blocks for each project day, with activities to rank in each, and the choices' status above](/bilder/en/wuensche.png)

Students open the registration and click **Submit or change choices**. For each project day, they see the time blocks and rank the activities in each: choice 1, choice 2 and so on. Every change is saved at once. The top shows whether their choices are complete, the period in which they were saved and the version number. An activity taking their whole class is marked “with your whole class”; they do not see activities that are not for their year or class.

## Registration status

![Registration status: how many students still lack complete choices, and each person's class, name, status and emails received](/bilder/en/stand-der-anmeldung.png)

From opening, the registration page shows everyone's progress with their choices: each person's class, status (no choices, incomplete, complete), period and emails received. **Registration status as Excel** provides the same list as a spreadsheet.

## Entering for someone

![Enter for someone: for whom, with the note that the entry is recorded with a reason, below it the choices, ranked by the organisation](/bilder/en/fuer-jemanden-eintragen.png)

**Enter** in a person's row opens their choices. Rank for them, write a **Reason** and click **Save choices**. SchulWahl records what you entered, with your reason.

## Exemptions

If a class activity takes a whole class, you can exempt someone in that class from it: their page lists every class activity under **Exemptions**, with the **Exempt** button. Once exempted with a reason, the person makes their own choices for that time. You can grant exemptions while registration is open.

## Late registration

If there is late registration, it starts automatically when the registration period ends. Anyone still without complete choices then receives a late registration invitation. Late registration choices count as late: the allocation places everyone else first and then, in a second round, those from late registration.

## Ending

Registration ends automatically at the end of the registration period or late registration. **End registration** ends it immediately; there is then no late registration. At the end, SchulWahl freezes the class lists for this registration. Anyone without complete choices then receives an email, and class teachers receive a list for their class. Anyone who has submitted no choices gets remaining places with no cost in the allocation.

## Emails during registration

| When | Who | Template |
| ---- | --- | -------- |
| on opening | all participating students | Registration invitation |
| one day before the registration period ends | anyone still without complete choices | Reminder before registration closes |
| at the end of the registration period | class teachers, with their class's list | Missing choices, to the class teacher |
| at the end of the registration period, if late registration follows | anyone still without complete choices | Late registration invitation |
| one day before late registration ends | anyone still without complete choices | Reminder before late registration closes |
| at the end of registration | anyone who has not submitted complete choices; class teachers again | Deadline missed; Missing choices, to the class teacher |

Change their texts under [Email templates](./e-mail-vorlagen).
