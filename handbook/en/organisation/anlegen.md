# Setting up and the catalogue

## Creating a registration

![The New registration form with name, school year and two project days](/bilder/en/anmeldung-anlegen.png)

Under **Registrations**, use **New registration** to enter the **Name** (Project Week 2027), **School year** (2026/27) and **Project days**; **Add day** adds another day. **Create registration** creates it in the “In preparation” state. Only the organisation can see it for now, and you can take your time entering the [settings](./einstellungen).

## Collecting activities

**Collect activities** opens the registration to teachers: they see it and submit their activities with **New activity**. The [Teachers](/en/lehrkraefte/aktivitaet) handbook explains the form. You can also create activities yourself, including for a teacher you enter in the form.

## Reviewing activities

![A registration's activities: a submitted activity with teacher, session, places and cost and the Edit, Approve, Return and Withdraw buttons](/bilder/en/aktivitaeten-pruefen.png)

The table shows each activity with its teachers, sessions, places, cost and status; the Organisation section counts drafts, submitted, approved and withdrawn activities. For a submitted activity you have three buttons:

- **Approve** adds it to the catalogue.
- **Return** sends it back with **Feedback to the teachers**. It becomes a draft again, and the teachers see your feedback.
- **Withdraw** removes it with a reason.

**Edit** opens any activity. Changes you make to a teacher's activity are recorded.

## Places and demand

![Places and demand: for each project day and year, how many students need a place and how many the activities offer](/bilder/en/bedarf.png)

For each project day and year, this shows how many students need a place and how many places the submitted and approved activities offer them. Anyone already in a class activity with their whole class is not counted. This shows you where more activities are needed. Teachers also see this table while activities are being collected.

## Publishing the catalogue

**Publish catalogue** closes submissions. The approved activities get their numbers and form the catalogue, which everyone can now see, including students. The name, school year and project days are now fixed.

![The catalogue: the registration name, project days and each activity's number, title, session, places, who it is for and cost](/bilder/en/katalog.png)

**View catalogue** shows it as students read it; **Print** prints it. **Catalogue as Excel** provides it as a spreadsheet ([Excel files](./excel)). You choose whether the catalogue names the teachers in the [settings](./einstellungen).
