# After publication

## What students see

![My allocation: activities or missing minutes for each project day, the cost and how the allocation was made](/bilder/en/meine-einteilung.png)

Under **View my allocation**, students see their activities for each project day, with clock times and how they got there: their choice, with their class or a remaining place. If they have missing minutes on a day, it says their class teacher will place them. Below are the total cost and how the allocation was made, with the draw number.

## The swap period

![Swap: activities with open places, one full activity, and what you leave for each one, with the Move button](/bilder/en/tauschen.png)

During the [swap period](./einstellungen#swap-period), students can move to an activity with open places. Places are first come, first served. A whole-class activity stays, and no one may have more missing time after the swap than before. For each activity, the page shows what you leave for it, or why you cannot move, such as “full”. A swap takes effect at once and is confirmed by email.

## Class teachers make placements

If someone has missing minutes on a day after publication, the class teacher places them in an activity with open places, with a reason ([As class teacher](/en/lehrkraefte/klassenvorstand)). The person receives an email, and you see the change with the reason.

## Changing the allocation by hand

![Changes since publication with kind, person, activities, reason and who made them, followed by the Change allocation by hand form](/bilder/en/aenderungen.png)

On the **Allocation** page, under **Published**, you see all **Changes since publication**: every swap, every placement by a class teacher and every change by hand, with the time, reason and who made it.

Below, you can change the allocation yourself: choose the person, the activity to **Remove from** and the one to **Place in** (either one is enough), write a reason and click **Change**. The same rules apply as for swaps: open places, a suitable year, no overlap with what stays, and a class activity always stays. You may override a full activity or a minimum with the checkbox **Even if the activity is full or too few would remain (recorded)**; the table marks this as “anyway”. The person receives an email.

## Activities after publication

You can still change an activity's title, description and location. Sessions, places, minimum, cost and who it is offered to stay as they are: they were used to calculate the allocation.

## Attendance

During project week, class teachers record their class's attendance ([As class teacher](/en/lehrkraefte/klassenvorstand#attendance)). **Attendance as Excel** on the Allocation page provides it for all classes.
