# Organisation

A **registration** takes the school from the first activities to the finished allocation for its project week. Anyone with the **Organisation** or **Administration** role creates and manages registrations; the administration grants the roles ([Accounts and roles](/en/verwaltung/konten-und-rollen)). A school year can have several registrations, such as a project week and a sports week.

## The process

Every registration goes through the same states. You take the next step with a button on the registration page; registration also starts and ends automatically at the times in the [settings](./einstellungen).

| Status | What happens | Continue with |
| ------ | ------------ | ------------- |
| In preparation | You create the registration; only the organisation can see it | **Collect activities** |
| Collecting activities | Teachers submit activities, you review them | **Publish catalogue** |
| Catalogue published | Everyone sees the numbered catalogue | **Open registration**, or automatically at the start |
| Registration open | Students submit their choices | automatically at the end, or **End registration** |
| Late registration | Anyone without complete choices submits them | automatically at the end of late registration, or **End registration** |
| Registration closed | The registration's class lists are frozen | **Calculate allocation** |
| Allocation in review | You review and compare calculations | **Publish** |
| Allocation published | Everyone sees their allocation; swaps, placements by class teachers, changes by hand | **Close registration** |
| Closed | Everything stays readable, nothing changes any more | – |

The steps in detail:

1. [Setting up and the catalogue](./anlegen)
2. [Settings](./einstellungen)
3. [During registration](./anmeldung)
4. [Allocation](./zuteilung)
5. [After publication](./nach-der-veroeffentlichung)
6. [Closing](./abschluss)

There are also the [Excel files](./excel) provided at each step, and the [email templates](./e-mail-vorlagen) for the emails SchulWahl sends along the way.

## A registration's page

![A registration page in preparation: name, school year and project days, followed by the Organisation section with the status and buttons](/bilder/en/anmeldung-in-vorbereitung.png)

Open each registration under **Registrations**. At the top are the school year, project days and, once set, the registration period, late registration, swap period and number of choices per time block. The **Organisation** section shows the status and button for the next step, plus the settings, catalogue as an Excel file and links to class teachers and email templates. Below, depending on the status, are the registration status, places and demand, and activities.

## Who sees what

- The **Organisation** sees every registration, in every state.
- **Teachers** see a registration once activities are being collected.
- **Students** see it once the catalogue is published. [During registration](./anmeldung) and [After publication](./nach-der-veroeffentlichung) show what they then see.
