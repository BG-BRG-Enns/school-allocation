# Organisation

A **registration** takes the school from the first activities to the finished allocation for its project week. Anyone with the **Organisation** or **Administration** role creates and manages registrations; the administration grants the roles ([Accounts and roles](/en/verwaltung/konten-und-rollen)). A school year can have several registrations, such as a project week and a sports week.

## The process

Every registration goes through the same states. You take the next step with a button on the registration page; every step is confirmed once more, with what it does and whether there is a way back. Registration also starts and ends automatically at the times in the [settings](./einstellungen).

![A step being confirmed: the Collect activities button, below it what the step does and the way back, and the Yes button](/bilder/en/schritt-bestaetigen.png)

| Status | What happens | Continue with | Back |
| ------ | ------------ | ------------- | ---- |
| In preparation | You create the registration; only the organisation can see it | **Collect activities** | **Delete registration** |
| Collecting activities | Teachers submit activities, you review them | **Publish catalogue** | **Back to preparation** |
| Catalogue published | Everyone sees the numbered catalogue | **Open registration**, or automatically at the start | **Back to collecting**; the numbers stay |
| Registration open | Students submit their choices | automatically at the end, or **End registration** | – |
| Late registration | Anyone without complete choices submits them | automatically at the end of late registration, or **End registration** | – |
| Registration closed | The registration's class lists are frozen | **Calculate allocation** | – |
| Allocation in review | You review and compare calculations | **Publish** | – |
| Allocation published | Everyone sees their allocation; swaps, placements by class teachers, changes by hand | **Close registration** | – |
| Closed | Everything stays readable, nothing changes any more | – | – |

From the opening on there is no way back: the invitations are out, the students are choosing.

The steps in detail:

1. [Setting up and the catalogue](./anlegen)
2. [Settings](./einstellungen)
3. [During registration](./anmeldung)
4. [Allocation](./zuteilung)

There are also the [Excel files](./excel) provided at each step, and the [email templates](./e-mail-vorlagen) for the emails SchulWahl sends along the way.

## A registration's page

![A registration page in preparation: name, school year and project days, followed by the Organisation section with the status and buttons](/bilder/en/anmeldung-in-vorbereitung.png)

Open each registration under **Registrations**. At the top are the school year, project days and, once set, the registration period, late registration, swap period and number of choices per time block. The **Organisation** section shows the status and button for the next step, plus the settings, catalogue as an Excel file and links to teachers by day, class teachers and email templates. Below, depending on the status, are the registration status, places and demand, and activities.

Long lists can be narrowed and folded: above the registration status, the activities, the teachers by day, the allocation and the import there are filters (a class, a state, a search word), and a heading with an arrow folds its section. The browser remembers both: a filter stays while you move between registration, class and allocation, and a folded section stays folded until you open it again. The counts (such as “12 of 700”) always count the whole list.

## Who sees what

- The **Organisation** sees every registration, in every state.
- **Teachers** see a registration once activities are being collected.
- **Students** see it once the catalogue is published. [During registration](./anmeldung) and [After publication](./nach-der-veroeffentlichung) show what they then see.
