# Closing

Once project week is over and attendance is recorded, close the registration: **Close registration** on its page, then confirm again. Nothing changes after that: no swaps, placements or attendance. Everything stays readable: the allocations, attendance and [Excel files](./excel).

First download what the school needs to keep, such as the allocation with the cost for the accounts department. The data is kept until one year after the end of the school year; the administration can then delete the school year ([Retention](/en/verwaltung/aufbewahrung)). Only a school year whose registrations are all closed can be deleted.
