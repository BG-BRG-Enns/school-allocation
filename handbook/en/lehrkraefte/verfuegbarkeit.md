# Availability

While the registration is **Collecting activities**, you enter on which project days you are there for an activity. To do so, click the **Enter my availability** button.

For each project day you choose:

- **available**: This holds as long as you enter nothing.
- **unavailable**: on a day off, say, or when you are needed elsewhere that day.
- **if needed**: You are there when it cannot be done without you.

SchulWahl saves your choice at once.

![My availability: a choice for each project day, here unavailable on Tuesday](/bilder/en/verfuegbarkeit.png)

When someone names you as a teacher of an activity, the form shows on which of its days you are unavailable or available only if needed ([Proposing an activity](./aktivitaet)). It can be submitted all the same; the organisation decides.

Once activities are no longer being collected, the days leave your list. If something changes later, tell the organisation.
