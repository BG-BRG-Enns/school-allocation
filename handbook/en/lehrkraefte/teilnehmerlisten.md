# Participants

Once the allocation is published, the **Activities** table has a **Participants** button for each of your activities. It opens the activity with the list of those placed in it. Above the list it says who leads the activity and who accompanies it.

![An activity's participants: each person with their class and how they joined, here by hand](/bilder/en/teilnehmerinnen.png)

Each person's entry shows their class and how they joined the activity: their choice, with their class, placed directly, a remaining place, a swap or by hand, through the class teacher or organisation. A swap or change by hand appears in the list at once.

## Placing after publication

Now too, under **Placed directly**, you place students into your activity and remove them again, and for a class activity you enter under **Not taking part** who does not come along after all, and reclaim whoever is placed elsewhere ([Submitting an activity](./aktivitaet#placing-students-directly)). Each of these changes the published allocation: the student leaves whatever overlaps the activity, including what the allocation gave them, receives an e-mail with their new programme, and the page tells you whom to tell. Nobody gives a reason; the organisation's list of changes says what it was.

## If an activity is cancelled

If an activity can be cancelled and too few students chose it to meet its minimum, it is cancelled during allocation. On publication, you then receive an email, and instead of the list it says: “This activity is cancelled: too few students chose it to meet its minimum.”

## Cost

Anyone placed pays the activity's cost. The organisation has the cost list for each person, in the allocation as an Excel file.
