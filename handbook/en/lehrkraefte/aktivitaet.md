# Proposing an activity

While a registration is **Collecting activities**, open it under **Registrations** and click **New activity**.

## Description and teachers

![The start of the New activity form: title, description, location or meeting point and teachers](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-beschreibung.png)

Enter the **Title**, **Description** and **Location or meeting point**. You are already listed under **Teachers**; add other teachers through the search. All teachers of an activity can edit it and later see its participants.

One of the teachers is the **lead**, the others are **companions**; below the teachers it says who leads and who accompanies. When you create an activity, you lead it. Whoever is added or signs up as a companion accompanies: the lead never changes by itself. If an activity has several teachers, you choose in the draft under **Lead** who leads it.

![The teachers of an activity: Carla Knoxbrunner and Bernd Pfnöxlhofer, below them lead and companions, the choice of the lead, the search and the number of companions](/bilder/en/leitung.png)

Once the activity is submitted or approved, you pass the lead on with **Hand the lead over to**, to one of its companions; if it should go to someone else, add that teacher as a companion first and save. You stay on it as a companion afterwards and can leave like any companion; the new lead then changes the companions. The activity stays as it was submitted.

![Handing over the lead: the choice of the companion the lead goes to, and the button Hand over the lead](/bilder/en/leitung-uebergeben.png)

If one of the teachers is unavailable on a day of the sessions, or available only if needed ([Availability](./verfuegbarkeit)), it says so there too. You can still save and submit; the organisation decides.

Under **Companions (0 to 8)** you say how many teachers should come along beside the lead. Add the ones you already know through the search; what is still missing then stands below the lead ("1 still sought") and on the list **Companions sought**, where teachers sign up themselves. For a submitted or approved activity you, as its lead, change only the companions and this number, with **Save companions**; everything else stays as the activity was submitted, and it stays approved.

### Companions sought

![Companions sought: per project day the activities that still seek a companion, with time, lead and the button I accompany](/bilder/en/begleitung-gesucht.png)

On the registration's page, **Companions sought** leads to a list per project day: the activities that still seek a companion and the ones you accompany, with time, lead, "Companions 1 of 2" and your availability on the day. A submitted activity is on it before its approval, marked "not approved yet". **I accompany** signs you up; from then on you are a teacher of the activity like a named one and later see its participants. It is not possible when you already lead or accompany something at the same time or are entered as unavailable on the day ("only if needed" is enough); the list tells you. If the companions have been found meanwhile, signing up tells you. Until registration opens you take yourself off with **Take me off**; after that tell the lead or the organisation, who take you out.

## Sessions

![The sessions: one session in periods, from period 2, lasting 4 periods, with its clock times](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-termine.png)

An activity usually has **one** session: one continuous span. It fits into the school's periods: choose the **Day**, **from period** and **Number of periods**; SchulWahl shows the clock times. For a trip or anything else outside the timetable, under **Given in**, choose **Clock time** and enter the start and end; such a session can also run over several days, from Tuesday 8:00 to Wednesday 13:00, say.

You do not enter how many minutes a session credits to students on each project day: SchulWahl counts the lesson time of the periods the session covers. Two periods of 50 minutes are 100 minutes, the break between them does not count; a trip from 8:00 to 13:00 counts the periods within that time, a period it cuts into by its minutes. What lies outside the timetable does not count; only a session wholly outside it, in the evening for example, counts by the clock. The credited minutes are shown in the catalogue and fill the minimum minutes students need each day.

**Further session (only for an interruption)** is for an activity that does not take place in one piece, such as two periods each on Monday and on Wednesday. Whoever is assigned attends every session. If the same activity is held again for other students, create it as an activity of its own.

## Participation

![Participation: whole classes, places, minimum, whether the activity can be cancelled, the years and classes it is offered to, followed by the cost](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-teilnahme.png)

- **Places**: the maximum number of students who can take part.
- **Minimum**: the minimum number needed.
- **Cancelled if too few**: With this ticked, the activity may be cancelled if too few students can be found to meet its minimum. Without the tick, it takes place, and the allocation must meet the minimum.
- **Offered to years (none selected: all)** and **Offered to classes (none selected: all)**: Without a selection, the activity is for everyone.

### A class activity

![A class activity: the whole of 5A takes part, the places are now called Extra places for other students, and the minimum is gone](/bilder/en/klassenaktivitaet.png)

If you select one or more classes under **Whole classes taking part (they make no choices for this time)**, it is a class activity: the whole classes take part and make no choices for that time. The places are then called **Extra places for other students**; other eligible students can choose these places, and 0 means there are none. They start at 0 until you enter a number. A class activity has no minimum and always takes place.

### An activity for teachers only

![An activity for teachers only: 0 places, no whole class, below it the note that students do not see it in the catalogue and nobody can choose it](/bilder/en/nur-lehrkraefte.png)

With **0 places** and without whole classes the activity is for its teachers only, a supervision for example: students do not see it in the catalogue, and nobody can choose it. The form says so below the places, the minimum is gone, and the table marks it "teachers only". Lead, companions and **Companions sought** work as for any activity, and the lock on new offers does not apply to it, since it offers no places.

## Placing students directly

![Placed directly: Sara Pfnäxthuber of 5A with the box ticked that she takes no part in class activities that overlap this one, below it the search for students](/bilder/en/direkt-eingeteilt.png)

Under **Placed directly** you enter students who come into the activity without choosing it: search by name or class and click **Place**. Every teacher of the activity can do this, from its first save and also once it is submitted or approved; for a new activity the list is entered when you save. Whoever is placed directly takes a place of the activity; an activity with 0 places can thus consist of placed students alone. An activity someone is placed into directly takes place: its minimum and **Cancelled if too few** no longer apply to it. In the activities table the places say how many are placed directly.

A direct placement comes before a class activity. With the box **takes no part in class activities that overlap this one**, which is ticked, the student takes part in no class activity of their class that overlaps your activity, and in none of it, its other days included: whoever does not go on the class trip is free for all its days and can choose or be placed into further activities. Beside the student it says which class activity that concerns and who leads it. Without the tick the student stays in their class activity and is only away during yours, for a rehearsal for example. SchulWahl asks nothing and notifies nobody: talk to the lead of the class activity.

If the student is already placed directly into another activity during this time, the later placement stands: they come to you, and the page tells you whom to tell. **Remove** takes a placement back.

### Not taking part

![Not taking part: for a class activity the students of its classes who are not in it, here Tim Gnöxwieser, entered, with the button takes part after all](/bilder/en/nimmt-nicht-teil.png)

For a class activity, **Not taking part** lists who of its classes is not in it. You enter someone through the search (**does not take part**) and take them off again with **takes part after all**; nobody gives a reason. You can do this as soon as the activity is saved and until registration closes; the class teacher and the organisation can too. Whoever is entered chooses for that time themselves or is placed directly, and Places and demand counts them as someone who needs a place.

If someone is listed there as **placed directly in** another activity, a teacher has taken them for that time. **Reclaim** takes that placement back, and the student is with you again; the page tells you whom to tell. The form of a new class activity already shows who of its classes is placed directly during that time.

If a class activity includes a night, the lists mark it "with an overnight stay", and the form reminds you to enter here who does not come along. The allocation never gives an activity with an overnight stay as a fallback.

## Cost

**Cost in euros (blank: none)** and **What for** appear in the catalogue. Anyone placed pays, so for a class activity its whole classes do. Otherwise the calculation places students in an activity with a cost only if they chose it, and never on a remaining place. After publication, a swap, the class teacher or the organisation can also bring someone into an activity with a cost.

## Categories

Under **Categories** you choose what the activity belongs to, such as sport or careers guidance; several or none are fine too. The organisation creates the categories for the whole school. A registration can have rules with them, such as “at least one sport activity”; students see the categories in the catalogue and with their choices.

## Saving and submitting

- **Save** keeps the activity as a draft. Only you, the activity's other teachers and the organisation see it.
- **Save and submit** submits it to the organisation.

![The lock in the form: the activity brings places only where the target is reached, the day on which every year has reached its target, and the advice to pick another time or add a year](/bilder/en/sperre.png)

Below the sessions the form says, as you fill it in, where the activity's places go: into which cells of Places and demand places are still missing, or that they go only where the target is reached already. If the organisation has turned on **Lock new offers**, the submission refuses an activity that goes only into cells that have reached the target: "On Mon, 21/06 every year of this activity has reached its target in P2 and P3." Then choose a time where places are still missing, or add a year that still lacks places; making it longer is welcome, as the extra places in a full period do no harm. Class activities are exempt from the lock. A class activity whose class is already in another submitted or approved class activity at the same time is refused and names it: "5A is already in 'Hike' (lead: ZwA) on Mon, 21/06 from 09:00 to 11:00." The form shows the conflict on the draft already; the organisation takes the class out of one activity, then you submit the other. If your activity is returned and you submit it again, the first submission still counts, and the lock checks only what you add in places, years, classes or times.

![The activities table with the filter Only mine: under the day it starts on, one's own activity, submitted and marked "teachers only"](/bilder/en/aktivitaeten-nach-tag.png)

After saving you are back on the registration's page, and the **Activities** table shows the saved activity marked. The table lists the activities under the day they start on, and there by their start: so you see what is already on offer that day. **Only mine** shows the activities you created or are a teacher of; **Order** sorts by number or by submission instead.

In the **Activities** table, a draft has the **Edit**, **Submit** and **Delete** buttons. You can take a submitted activity **Back to draft** while activities are being collected. If something is wrong, the form says what, such as “A session is outside the project days” or “The minimum is greater than the number of places”.

If the organisation returns your activity, it becomes a draft again, and the **Feedback from the organisation** appears at the top. Change what it mentions and submit it again. Approved activities enter the catalogue when it is published.

## Places and demand

![Places and demand: a table per project day, the years as rows, Monday with its attendance frame a column per period, with need, target and offer](/bilder/en/bedarf.png)

While activities are being collected, the registration page and the activity form show under **Places and demand**, for each project day and year, how many students need a place, the **target** and how many places the submitted and approved activities offer. If a day has an attendance frame, that is shown per period; an activity counts in every period it touches. The target lies above the need by a share the organisation sets, usually 20%, so the choices have room. A cell short of the target is marked; another activity helps most there, and that is where a session is worth placing.
