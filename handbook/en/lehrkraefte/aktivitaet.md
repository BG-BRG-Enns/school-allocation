# Proposing an activity

While a registration is **Collecting activities**, open the project week under **Registrations** and click **New activity**. As you fill in the form, it shows you what is still missing.

## Description and teachers

![The start of the New activity form: title, description, location or meeting point and the teachers](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-beschreibung.png)

Enter the **Title**, **Description** and **Location or meeting point**. You are already listed under **Teachers**; add other teachers through the search. All teachers of an activity can edit it and later see who takes part.

Whoever creates the activity is its **lead**; all the others are **companions**. With several teachers, you choose under **Lead** who leads. After submitting, you pass the lead on to a companion with **Hand the lead over to**.

![The teachers of an activity: Carla Knoxbrunner and Bernd Pfnöxlhofer, below them lead and companions, the choice of the lead, the search and the number of companions](/bilder/en/leitung.png)

![Handing over the lead: the choice of the companion the lead goes to, and the button Hand over the lead](/bilder/en/leitung-uebergeben.png)

Under **Number of accompanying teachers wanted** you enter how many teachers should come along. If someone is still missing, the activity appears under **Companions sought**. If a teacher is not [available](./verfuegbarkeit) on a day, the form shows it. You can submit all the same; the organisation decides.

### Companions sought

![Companions sought: per project day the activities that still seek a companion, with time, lead and the button Join as an accompanying teacher](/bilder/en/begleitung-gesucht.png)

**Companions sought** on the registration's page lists, per project day, the activities that still seek companions. **Join as an accompanying teacher** signs you up. You can take yourself off there until the students start choosing; after that, turn to the lead.

## Sessions

![The sessions: one session in periods, from period 2, lasting 4 periods, with its clock times](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-termine.png)

Choose the **Day**, **from period** and **Number of periods**. For a trip, choose **Clock time** under **Given in** and enter the start and end, over several days too, from Tuesday 8:00 to Wednesday 13:00, say.

If the activity is interrupted, such as two periods each on Monday and on Wednesday, add **Another session for the same group**. Whoever takes part attends every session. If the activity is held again for other students, create a new activity.

If your activity is shorter than the minimum length the organisation has set, the form shows how much you must lengthen it: "Please lengthen the activity on Wed, 23/06 by 15 minutes: between 8:30 and 12:15 it must last at least 100 minutes."

![Below the sessions: please lengthen the activity by enough minutes to reach the minimum length on this day](/bilder/en/mindestlaenge.png)

## Participation

![Participation: whole classes, the maximum and minimum number of participants, whether the activity may be cancelled, the years and classes it is offered to, followed by the cost](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-teilnahme.png)

- **Maximum number of participants**: the most students who can take part.
- **Minimum number of participants**: the fewest there should be.
- **May be cancelled …**: With this ticked, the activity is cancelled if the allocation does not reach the minimum number. So tick it only if no cancellation fees or similar would apply. Without the tick, it takes place for certain.
- **Offered to years** and **Offered to classes**: without a selection, for everyone.

**With the whole class**: If you choose **Whole classes taking part**, these classes come along as a whole, and the activity takes place for certain. Under **Extra places for other students** you offer free places to others, free seats on the coach, say.

![An activity with the whole class: 5A takes part, Extra places for other students replaces the maximum number of participants, and the minimum number is gone](/bilder/en/klassenaktivitaet.png)

**Extra**: If the activity lies wholly outside the attendance time, such as a theatre visit in the evening, it is an extra. Students do not choose it; you place them under **Placed directly**.

**Teachers only**: With 0 under **Maximum number of participants** and without whole classes, the activity is for teachers only, such as a supervision. Students do not see it.

![An activity for teachers only: a maximum of 0 participants, no whole class, below it the note that students do not see it](/bilder/en/nur-lehrkraefte.png)

## Placing students directly

![Placed directly: Sara Pfnäxthuber of 5A with the box ticked that she takes no part at all in overlapping class activities, below it the search for students](/bilder/en/direkt-eingeteilt.png)

Under **Placed directly** you bring individual students into your activity without a choice, for a performance, say: search by name or class and click **Place**. The activity then takes place for certain.

If the student has a class activity at the same time, the tick decides: with the tick, they take no part at all in the class activity, not on its other days either. Without the tick, they are only away from it during your activity. Let the lead of the class activity know. The form shows who that is.

### Not taking part

![Not taking part: for a class activity the students of its classes who are not in it, here Tim Gnöxwieser, entered, with the button takes part after all, below it the search for students of the classes](/bilder/en/nimmt-nicht-teil.png)

For an activity with the whole class, you enter under **Not taking part** who does not come along: find the name, **does not take part**. This works in a new activity before you save it, and until the organisation calculates the allocation; **takes part after all** brings the student back in. If the activity includes a night, the form reminds you. If someone has taken a student of the class directly into another activity, it says so here, and **Return … to this class activity** brings them back to you.

## Cost and categories

**Cost in euros** and **What for** appear in the catalogue. Anyone placed pays. Under **Categories** you choose what the activity belongs to, such as sport or careers guidance.

## Saving and submitting

- **Save** keeps the activity as a draft. Only its teachers and the organisation see it.
- **Save and submit** sends it to the organisation.
- If you leave the page with changes that are not saved, the browser asks first.

![The lock in the form: at this time there are already enough places for these years, and the advice to choose another time or to offer the activity to another year as well](/bilder/en/sperre.png)

Below the sessions the form shows whether your activity helps where places are still missing. If there are already enough places at its time, the organisation can lock submitting; then choose another time or offer the activity to a year that still lacks places as well.

If the organisation returns your activity, its **Feedback from the organisation** is at the top of the form. Change what it mentions and submit it again.

![The activities table with the filter Only mine: under the day it starts on, one's own activity, submitted and marked "teachers only"](/bilder/en/aktivitaeten-nach-tag.png)

The **Activities** table shows what is already on offer on each day. **Only mine** shows only yours. Withdrawn activities are not in it; you find them by choosing "All" or "Withdrawn" under **State**.

## Places and demand

![Places and demand: a table per project day, the years as rows, Monday with its attendance frame a column per period, with need, target and offer](/bilder/en/bedarf.png)

**Places and demand** shows, for each project day and year, how many students still need a place and how many places are offered. The cells where places are still missing are marked: a new activity helps most there.
