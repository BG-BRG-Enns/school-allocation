# Proposing an activity

While a registration is **Collecting activities**, open it under **Registrations** and click **New activity**.

## Description and teachers

![The start of the New activity form: title, description, location or meeting point and teachers](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-beschreibung.png)

Enter the **Title**, **Description** and **Location or meeting point**. You are already listed under **Teachers**; add other teachers through the search. All teachers of an activity can edit it and later see its participants.

Whoever created the activity is its **lead** as long as they are among its teachers; otherwise the first teacher named is. The others are **companions**. Below the teachers it says who leads and who accompanies. It changes nobody's rights.

If one of the teachers is unavailable on a day of the sessions, or available only if needed ([Availability](./verfuegbarkeit)), it says so there too. You can still save and submit; the organisation decides.

## Sessions

![The sessions: one session in periods, from period 2, lasting 4 periods, with clock times and credited minutes on the project day](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-termine.png)

A session fits into the school's periods: choose the **Day**, **from period** and **Number of periods**; SchulWahl shows the clock times. For a trip or anything else outside the timetable, under **Given in**, choose **Clock time** and enter the start and end.

**Minutes on** each project day are the minutes the session credits to students on that day. Travel and breaks do not count. Credited minutes fill the minimum minutes students need each day. **Add session** gives an activity several sessions, for example on two days.

## Participation

![Participation: whole classes, places, minimum, whether the activity can be cancelled, the years and classes it is offered to, followed by the cost](/bilder/en/aktivitaet-teilnahme.png)

- **Places**: the maximum number of students who can take part.
- **Minimum**: the minimum number needed.
- **Cancelled if too few**: With this ticked, the activity may be cancelled if too few students can be found to meet its minimum. Without the tick, it takes place, and the allocation must meet the minimum.
- **Offered to years (none selected: all)** and **Offered to classes (none selected: all)**: Without a selection, the activity is for everyone.

### A class activity

![A class activity: the whole of 5A takes part, the places are now called Extra places for other students, and the minimum is gone](/bilder/en/klassenaktivitaet.png)

If you select one or more classes under **Whole classes taking part (they make no choices for this time)**, it is a class activity: the whole classes take part and make no choices for that time. The places are then called **Extra places for other students**; other eligible students can choose these places, and 0 means there are none. A class activity has no minimum and always takes place.

## Cost

**Cost in euros (blank: none)** and **What for** appear in the catalogue. Anyone placed pays, so for a class activity its whole classes do. Otherwise the calculation places students in an activity with a cost only if they chose it, and never on a remaining place. After publication, a swap, the class teacher or the organisation can also bring someone into an activity with a cost.

## Saving and submitting

- **Save** keeps the activity as a draft. Only you, the activity's other teachers and the organisation see it.
- **Save and submit** submits it to the organisation.

In the **Activities** table, a draft has the **Edit**, **Submit** and **Delete** buttons. You can take a submitted activity **Back to draft** while activities are being collected. If something is wrong, the form names the rule, such as “A session is outside the project days” or “The minimum is greater than the number of places”.

If the organisation returns your activity, it becomes a draft again, and the **Feedback from the organisation** appears at the top. Change what it mentions and submit it again. Approved activities enter the catalogue when it is published.

## Places and demand

![Places and demand: for each project day and year, how many students need a place, the target and how many are offered](/bilder/en/bedarf.png)

While activities are being collected, the registration page shows, for each project day and year, how many students need a place, the **target** and how many places the submitted and approved activities offer. The target lies above the need by a share the organisation sets, usually 20%, so the choices have room. A year short of the target is marked; another activity helps most there.
