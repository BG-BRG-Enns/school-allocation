# As class teacher

If you are a class teacher, a registration's page has the **My class** button with your class's name, such as **My class 5A**. You become a class teacher through the administration or organisation, or because the class list names you.

## Before registration

![The class page before registration: the class activities and the class's students](/bilder/en/meine-klasse.png)

The page shows your class's students and the class activities taking the whole class.

## During registration

![The class's students during registration, each with the status of their choices](/bilder/en/klasse-anmeldestand.png)

Once registration is open, each person's progress is shown: no choices, incomplete or complete. At the end of the registration period, you receive an email listing everyone in your class whose choices are still missing, and another at the end of registration. Speak to them about it; the organisation can also enter choices for someone.

## After publication: placing those short of time

![Not placed for every day: a person with missing minutes, a choice of an activity with open places, a reason and the Place button](/bilder/en/klasse-einteilen.png)

On publication, you receive an email listing everyone in your class not yet placed for every day. Under **Not placed for every day**, each of these people is listed, with their missing minutes and what they have so far.

1. Choose an activity that fits the gap. The selection shows each one's open places; one that is not possible, for example because it is full, is listed with the reason and cannot be selected.
2. Write a **Reason**.
3. Click **Place**.

The person receives an email, and the organisation sees the change with your reason. If no activity fits the gap, it says so; the organisation can then change the allocation.

## Attendance

![Attendance on a project day: present, absent or excused for each person and session, with a note, and the Save attendance button](/bilder/en/anwesenheit.png)

Under **Attendance**, enter whether the students in your class were **present**, **absent** or **excused** for each project day and session, optionally with a note, and click **Save attendance**. You can make corrections until the registration is closed; a recorded attendance entry can be changed, but cannot be cleared again. **Attendance as Excel** provides your class's list. After closing, everything is read-only.
